PRN Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2025

PRN ("the app") is a clinical decision support tool for pharmacists, pharmacy students, and healthcare providers. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

PRN collects no personal information. Specifically:

  • No name, email address, or account is required to use the app.
  • No health or medical information is collected or stored on our servers.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

PRN stores the following data locally on your device only using Apple's AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Your display name or nickname (optional, set by you)
  • Your selected rotation preference
  • Recently accessed tools
  • Lookup count (for display purposes only)

You can clear this data at any time by deleting and reinstalling the app.

Drug Interaction Queries

When you use the Drug Interactions feature, the names of the medications you enter are sent to our secure server at pilltalk.app to generate a clinical interaction summary. No personally identifiable information is transmitted — only the drug names you type. These queries are not stored or logged.

Third-Party Services

PRN uses Google Gemini (via our backend server) to power the Drug Interactions feature. Drug names are processed to generate clinical summaries. No personal data is shared with Google.

Children

PRN is intended for healthcare professionals and students. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
