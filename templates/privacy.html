Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2025

Pill Talk ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the Pill Talk app and website (pilltalk.app) handles your information.

Information We Do Not Collect

Pill Talk does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not require you to create an account. We do not track your usage. We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any other identifying information.

Information Stored on Your Device

Pill Talk stores the following information locally on your device only, using your browser's local storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Your saved medication list
  • Your dose tracker entries
  • Your side effect log
  • Your emergency medication card details

Medication Lookups

When you search for a medication, the drug name and your selected reading level are sent to our server to generate a response using an AI language model (Google Gemini). We do not store these queries or associate them with any user identity.

Third-Party Services

Pill Talk uses Google Gemini AI to generate medication information. Please review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children's Privacy

Pill Talk does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL.
